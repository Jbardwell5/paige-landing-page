Platform Architecture

Software Purpose-Built for the Frontline

Deploy PAiGE on your existing fleet through BYOD or leverage our dedicated industrial client. A unified communications OS engineered for high-noise, zero-latency plant environments.

Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Run PAiGE natively across iOS, Android, and enterprise mobile platforms. Turn standard smartphones or existing rugged mobile devices into instant push-to-talk field radios.

MDM & Zero-Touch Setup

Push software configurations instantly across thousands of endpoints using Knox, SOTI, Intune, or AirWatch. No manual worker configuration required on the floor.

Web Dispatch Command Center

Give plant supervisors and dispatchers real-time visibility. Broadcast site-wide emergency alerts, manage channels, and track active floor tickets from any standard browser.

Store-and-Forward Sync

Operating in RF-shielded zones or subterranean floor space? PAiGE buffers voice transmissions and task state locally, syncing automatically upon network re-connection.

Enterprise Grade Security

End-to-end encrypted audio streams and data transmission ensure operational communications remain secure, fully compliant, and auditable across shift logs.

Industrial Webhook APIs

Connect PAiGE directly to SCADA systems, PLC fault alarms, or ERP task queues to trigger automatic voice announcements and dispatch alerts to specific work zones.
